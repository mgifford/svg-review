Default is Auto (actual use) on a checkerboard so white and black artwork both remain visible. The Compare dialog shows uncompressed SVG source and a unified line diff. It also lists before/after file size and viewBox. “Literal refs” is a conservative count, not proof of use. The after asset is not hidden when there is a missing counterpart: a dashed red placeholder states it explicitly.

CurrentColor: unused icons are shown on the checkerboard and labelled unused — no consumer; the checker means transparency, not a white background. They have no dark-mode rendering. Inline currentColor is previewed as a mask because it inherits text colour. An background currentColor is a real failure: the background image resolves to black, and is marked in red. See the CurrentColor dark-mode audit for all 18 listed assets.

White inverse pretints (check-inverse, chevron-down-inverse, remove-inverse) are shown on a dark plate so their white strokes remain visible. They are designed for dark, selected or forced-dark surfaces. In forced-light Drupal, the checkbox reverts to the native control, the select removes its background image, and the media-library remove button uses the dark glyph instead.
